Tiered autonomy
Most incidents resolve invisibly. Only what needs a human surfaces.
Tier 1
Silent
Fully reversible, no SLA exposure, no safety flag — executes end to end, nobody gets pinged.
Tier 2
Auto + notify
Acts on its own, then tells the operator what it did.
Tier 3
Human approves
Degraded confidence, SLA-breach risk, or safety involvement — one decision card, then it waits.
Most incidents are invisible. That restraint is what gives an escalation its weight.
